# Example — `saas-launch-kit`

A real run of the `saas-launch-kit` skill, abridged to the load-bearing sections.

## Input

> "Inboxly — an AI inbox triage tool for solo founders. Differentiator: it drafts
> replies in *your* voice, learned from your past sent mail. Want to launch on
> Product Hunt in 3 weeks. Pricing intent: free tier + paid. Channels: PH, X, an
> indie-hacker community."

---

## Output (abridged)

# SaaS Launch Kit — Inboxly

## 1. Positioning
- **Statement:** For solo founders drowning in email, Inboxly is the AI inbox
  assistant that drafts replies in your actual voice. Unlike generic AI email
  tools, Inboxly learns from your sent mail — so drafts sound like you, not a robot.
- **One-liner:** Your inbox, triaged. Your replies, in your voice.
- **Headline:** "Your inbox, triaged. Your replies, in your voice."
  **Subhead:** "Inboxly reads your sent mail to learn how you write, then drafts
  replies that actually sound like you. Approve in one click."
- **Value pillars:**
  1. Drafts in *your* voice — not generic AI tone.
  2. Triage that surfaces what needs you, hides what doesn't.
  3. One-click approve — you stay in control, you just move faster.
- **Objections & responses:**
  | Objection | Response |
  |---|---|
  | "AI email sounds robotic" | It learns from *your* sent mail — that's the whole point. |
  | "I don't want it sending without me" | It never sends. It drafts; you approve. |
  | "Privacy?" | Your mail is processed, never stored long-term, never used to train shared models. |

## 2. Landing Page Copy (sections)
- **Hero:** headline + subhead above, demo GIF, single CTA "Try Inboxly free".
- **Social proof:** "Trusted by 200 founders in private beta" + 2 named quotes.
- **Problem:** "You spend 2 hours a day in your inbox. Most of it is replies you
  could write in your sleep — if you had the time."
- **Solution / Pillars / How it works (3 steps) / Pricing teaser / FAQ / Final CTA.**

## 3. Pricing & Packaging
| Tier | Price | For whom | Gates |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | Trying it out | 50 drafts/mo |
| Pro | $19/mo | Solo founders | Unlimited drafts + voice tuning |
| Team | $49/seat/mo | Small teams | Shared voice profiles + admin |

- **Anchor logic:** Pro is the intended default; Free gates *volume*, never the
  core "voice" magic — so the magic is what converts.
- **Trial strategy:** Free is the trial. No credit card. Upgrade prompt at the
  50-draft ceiling.

## 4. Onboarding Flow
- **Activation metric:** first approved draft within 24h of signup.
- **Steps:** connect inbox → Inboxly learns voice from last 200 sent → first 3
  drafts shown → user approves one ("aha") → daily triage habit.

## 5. Lifecycle Emails
| # | Trigger | Subject | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Signup | "Inboxly is learning how you write" | Set expectation |
| 2 | First draft ready | "Your first drafts are ready to review" | Drive activation |
| 3 | Day 3, no approval | "Still sounds like you? Take a look" | Recover activation |
| 4 | Hit free limit | "You've used all 50 drafts — here's Pro" | Convert |
| 5 | Day 14 inactive | "We saved your voice profile" | Win-back |

## 6. Launch Sequence
- **Pre-launch (T-14 → T-1):** waitlist live, weekly build-in-public X posts,
  warm up the indie-hacker community, line up 5 hunters/supporters.
- **Launch day (PH):**
  - 12:01am PT — PH post goes live.
  - 6:00am — founder X thread with the demo GIF.
  - 9:00am — post in the indie-hacker community (value-first, not a drop-and-run).
  - All day — reply to every PH comment within 15 min.
  - 5:00pm — "we're #N" update across channels.
- **Post-launch (T+1 → T+14):** thank-you to every supporter, retarget waitlist
  non-converters, publish the launch retro as content.

## 7. SEO & Content
- **Core pages:** /, /pricing, /how-it-works, /privacy, /blog.
- **First content pieces:** "How to write email faster as a solo founder",
  "AI email tools compared", "What your sent mail says about your writing voice".
- **Legal pages checklist:** Privacy Policy, Terms, DPA, sub-processor list.

## 8. Analytics
- **North-star:** weekly approved drafts per active user.
- **Guardrail:** draft edit-rate (if users heavily edit drafts, the voice model
  is failing — that's the metric that protects the core promise).
- **Funnel:** visit → signup → inbox connected → first draft → first approval →
  week-2 retained.

## Launch Readiness Checklist
- [ ] Positioning locked, every asset traces to it
- [ ] One primary CTA across all surfaces
- [ ] Pricing page live with anchored tiers
- [ ] Onboarding hits activation metric for a test cohort
- [ ] Lifecycle emails wired and triggering
- [ ] Legal pages published
- [ ] Analytics funnel instrumented end-to-end
- [ ] 5 launch-day supporters confirmed
